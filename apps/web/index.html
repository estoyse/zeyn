Real-time multiplayer trivia quiz games.

Zeyn lets you host or join a live quiz room, buzz in to answer questions against other players, and watch scores update in real time. Games are free to play, and you can play in Uzbek, Russian or English. You can browse the games and play as a guest without creating an account.
